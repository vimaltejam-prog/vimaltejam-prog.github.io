Payments projects

Verifone · PIN debit routing

I led PIN debit routing requirements for Commander Payments to help U.S. fuel and convenience retailers lower processing costs and improve payment acceptance. Lower fees give retailers room to pass savings on to shoppers.

HSBC · UPI integration

I supported HSBC’s integration with India’s Unified Payments Interface (UPI) so large merchants could offer customer-to-customer transfers.

Research informed by practice

At the intersection of payments
and trustworthy systems.

I work as a Business Analyst and Product Owner at Verifone, contributing to payment systems and platform modernization. I hold a master’s degree in Information Technology and Management from The University of Texas at Dallas, where I received the Scholar with Distinction honor.

My research focuses on the decisions and architectures behind dependable financial infrastructure: identifying fraud, limiting sensitive data exposure, routing transactions, and preparing payment gateways for new cryptographic risks.
